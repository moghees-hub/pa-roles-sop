Why

Why this role

With Harry gone and Callum on marketing, nobody is holding the numbers together. This puts one person on it, with a fixed weekly rhythm.

George: "Head of operations, analytics, data, everything all in one place. Bring the data, hold people accountable to that data, bring it all together, and each week we go through it."
The role

Head of Operations and Analytics: what I own

  • One scorecardEvery number the business runs on, in one place, locked every Monday for the week before.
  • AccountabilityEach number has an owner and a target. When it is off target, the owner brings the reason and the fix.
  • Weekly callsI run a short call with each area (lead gen, sales, marketing, revenue) and a weekly review with George and Callum.
  • The systems behind itI keep the dashboards and automations that feed the numbers accurate and running, and build what is missing.

Rules

How accountability works

  1. Every number has one ownerNot a team. One name, so it is clear who answers for it.
  2. Every number has a targetSet by George. Green on target, amber close, red off target.
  3. Red means a reason and a fixThe owner brings both to their weekly call, not just the number.
  4. Every fix gets an actionWritten down with an owner and a deadline, checked the following week.
  5. Repeat reds go to GeorgeTwo weeks red in a row comes up in the weekly review.
