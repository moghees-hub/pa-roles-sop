Overview

I look after the technical and operational side of Physique Academy: the systems the business runs on, the payments set-up across our three Stripe accounts, the marketing and tracking stack, the website, design work and the clothing operation. On top of that I'm the day-to-day point of contact for the coaching team.

This page is set out so you can read it at two levels. Sections 1 to 3 give the big picture: how everything connects, what runs when, and what I check. Section 4 is the reference part: one card per system saying what it does, who uses it, when it runs and what happens if it stops.

Hours and availability

My work runs across a long window rather than fixed office hours. Coach queries, client issues and system problems come in at all hours and across time zones, so I stay reachable outside normal working time and deal with anything urgent when it happens rather than the next morning.

Section 1

How it all connects: from a booked call to a looked-after client

This is what happens for a single client, start to finish. I built every step and I'm across it every day.

  1. A lead books a callThey land on one of the landing or booking pages I built and pick a slot on the GoHighLevel calendar for that channel. Their intake answers and where they came from are saved against their contact.
  2. The team is told instantlyThe New Bookings bot posts a card into #new-bookings with the lead's details and answers, and DMs the assigned salesperson so they can prepare.
  3. It shows in the morning briefingAt 9am the Daily Stats bot posts that day's confirmed calls per salesperson into #sales, each one linked to its booking card.
  4. The call happensThe salesperson marks the outcome with one emoji on the booking card (closed, thinking, lost, no-show, cancelled) and the GoHighLevel pipeline moves by itself. If the call is recorded in Fathom, an AI summary is posted for review.
  5. The sale is madePayment goes through Stripe (UK, US or UAE). From that one payment: a sale alert posts to Slack, the Sales Dashboard records it, the Booked Calls Tracker matches it back to the call, the Commission Tracker credits the salesperson, and Incoming Revenue counts it as new cash in GBP.
  6. The client gets a coachThe client is allocated to a coach on Trello. The coach gets an email with the client's details, the client gets their welcome email, and the Master Tracker and ChartMogul are updated, all by my Coach Allocation system (which replaced the old Zaps and ActiveCampaign automation).
  7. End of dayAt 11:55pm the Daily Stats bot posts the end-of-day sales report: what was sold, by whom, for how much.
  8. From then on, the systems look after themThey appear in their coach's My Clients list with live payment status. Milestones flag them at 3 and 5 months. A failed payment alerts the team straight away. If they're due to cancel, the coach sees a warning. Their history feeds the retention reports and the weekly and monthly client tallies.

Section 3

What I do day to day

  • Morning checksConfirm the overnight and morning reports went out and the numbers look right. If one is missing or wrong, I find out why and re-send it.
  • Coach and head coach supportI'm the first point of contact for the coaches and head coaches all day, every day: client account queries, access problems, chasing requests, and help with any of our systems.
  • Requests inboxWork through Coach Hub submissions (plan changes, pauses, issues, holidays) and make sure each one reaches the right person and gets actioned.
  • PaymentsWatch failed payments, disputes and cancellations as they come in; set up payment links, plan changes, free months and moves between Stripe accounts.
  • Fixes and changesFix anything that breaks the same day. Platforms like Stripe, Slack, GoHighLevel and Fathom change how they work without warning, and that silently breaks things if nobody is watching.
  • New workBuild the new systems, reports and pages you and the team ask for (see Section 15 for what's in progress).
